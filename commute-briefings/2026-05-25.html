An 8B Model Just Beat 355B-Parameter Giants — Using 95% Fewer Thinking Tokens

CMU and MBZUAI's SR²AM (Self-Regulated Simulative Reasoning Agentic LLM) decomposes agentic reasoning into three systems: a world model that runs forward simulations before acting, a self-regulation layer that controls planning scope and depth, and a reactive execution engine for real-time tool calls. The 8B variant achieves Pass@1 scores competitive with systems 44× larger (120–355B parameters), while the 30B variant uses 25.8–95.3% fewer reasoning tokens than comparably-sized agentic LLMs. Reinforcement learning increased the model's planning horizon by 22.8% while increasing planning frequency by only 2% — meaning it thinks deeper, not more often.

AI Security

Samsung Invented a Way to Lock AI Model Weights — No Retraining, No Dataset Needed

Samsung Research's LoREnc is a training-free encryption framework securing foundation models and LoRA adapters through three mathematical operations: spectral truncation that suppresses dominant low-rank components in weight matrices; compensation injection into authorized adapter layers to restore suppressed information; and orthogonal reparameterization that erases structural fingerprints exploitable by model-extraction attacks. Unauthorized users receive structurally collapsed outputs indistinguishable from noise, while authorized users recover exact performance — all at under 1% additional computational overhead. No retraining or original dataset access is required, making it deployable on already-distributed weights.

60-Second Hook

What if someone downloading your model weights got back pure noise — but your authorized users got perfect performance? Samsung just built a mathematical lock for AI that works even after the weights are already in the wild.

Mind-Bending Theory

Your Brain and a Stranger's Brain Share Identical Hidden Geometry — AI Just Proved It

Researchers at the Universitat de Barcelona applied self-supervised learning to fMRI scans and discovered that the human visual cortex has a universal geometric structure that is approximately isometric across individuals — meaning two people's neural representation spaces are identical up to a simple orthogonal rotation (the same operation as rotating a coordinate system). Without any paired data or cross-subject labels, they showed these subject-specific spaces can be aligned to a shared common coordinate system through unsupervised matrix decomposition alone. This "Platonic geometry" of perception has direct implications for brain-computer interfaces, neural decoding generalization, and understanding the mathematical foundations of consciousness.

60-Second Hook

Two strangers' brains process vision using the exact same hidden geometry — just rotated differently. AI found the Rosetta Stone of human perception, and it turns out it's made entirely of math.

Someone Used AI to Reconstruct a Dead Pilot's Voice From a Mathematical Image

Following the UPS Flight 2976 crash investigation, individuals discovered that the NTSB's public docket contained a spectrogram — a mathematical image representation of cockpit audio — which federal law doesn't prohibit publishing. Using the spectrogram image alongside the publicly available accident transcript, they employed AI tools including Codex to reverse-engineer approximations of the actual pilot voices. The NTSB temporarily shut down its entire public docket system and locked 42 active investigations pending review, exposing a fundamental regulatory gap: safety data published in one form (mathematical image) can be reconstructed into another form (audio) that was legally prohibited from publication.

Real-Time AI Music Improvisation Now Runs on a Consumer Gaming Laptop

Live Music Diffusion Models (LMDMs) adapt standard audio diffusion architectures for interactive, real-time music generation through two key breakthroughs: block-wise Key-Value Caching that recovers the inference efficiency of discrete autoregressive models while maintaining diffusion output quality, and ARC-Forcing — a post-training paradigm that eliminates error accumulation in block-wise generation without requiring explicit reinforcement learning or reward models. The system achieves real-time latency on consumer gaming laptops and was demonstrated in live artist-AI collaboration as a "generative delay" effect, enabling dynamic timbral manipulation and harmonic response during live performance — essentially a co-improvising AI bandmate.
